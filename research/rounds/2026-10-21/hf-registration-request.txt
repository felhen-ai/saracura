Hello Hugging Face evaluation team,

We maintain felhen-ai/ptbr-typed-decisions-bench: seven closed-decision tasks on Brazilian Portuguese text, 5,196 test items, with balanced accuracy per task and an unweighted mean. The dataset is public, with source licenses documented. The evaluator shuffles option order deterministically and publishes per-item traces and checkpoint provenance. We have an auditable complete CPU reproduction with upstream Laya and are preparing a 14-model local round.

Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/felhen-ai/ptbr-typed-decisions-bench
